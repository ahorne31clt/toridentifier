Tornado Guesser v1.8iOS TestFlight testing is here · November 27, 2026
  • iOS testing: Tornado Guesser v1.8 is available through TestFlight for testing. The full App Store launch is coming soon.
  • Replay Mode: signed-in players unlock other players’ five round maps after saving the same day’s Daily Challenge. Choose a player, step through the rounds, and review the final rundown.
  • Share every chase: share full tornado rundowns from Photo, Iconic, Past Year, and EF Rating modes. Daily shares stay spoiler-free.
  • Updated branding: new tornado-search logo, muted red accents, and slimmer mobile-friendly navigation.
  • Sign in your way: play as a guest or create an account with a username and six-digit PIN. Save your recovery code, and sign in across browsers to access your score history.
  • Your gamertag, connected: claim an available guest username to link its stored leaderboard results to your account.
  • Better EF scoring: 5 points exact, 3 one level away, 1 two levels away, and 0 farther away. Five photos, 25 possible points. EF-U earns exact-match credit only; earlier scores remain in account history.
  • A new visual direction: square surfaces, distinct tornado-photo mode cards, moving tracking targets, circular radar sweeps, and full-width mobile mode cards.
  • Guess the EF Rating: the green five-photo event-recognition challenge, with hints and its own points leaderboard.
  • Daily chase board: purple Daily feature, player EF ranks, five round grades, and aligned placement and mileage boxes. Find and share your Daily score.
  • Run rundowns, automatic reconnection, and a header that scrolls away keep the experience focused on the game.
