Tornado Guesser v1.611.6.1 · Bug fix update · November 25, 2026

1.6.1 · Bug fixes and leaderboard improvements

  • Daily round grades: five colored A/B/C/D/F badges show each player’s result for rounds 1–5, on the homepage and full Daily leaderboard.
  • Daily leaderboard EF ranks: the homepage chase board now shows each player’s EF rank beside their gamertag, based on their Daily average distance.
  • Corrected the ranked-player average label when only one player has saved a Daily score.

Also included · 1.6 updates

  • A refreshed chase board: animated weather contours, matching cards, and clearer rankings.
  • Guess the EF Rating: a new green, five-photo challenge with its own shared points leaderboard.
  • Your run rundown: a results screen for every game mode, with all five rounds.
  • Daily Challenge takes the spotlight: purple featured panel with today’s live rankings.
  • Feedback, score lookup, accounts, daily sharing, and the classic contour background remain available.
