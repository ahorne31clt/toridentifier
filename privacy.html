Tornado Guesser

Privacy policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

This policy describes information handled by the Tornado Guesser website and iPhone app, operated by the Tornado Guesser team, Christopher Horne and Blake Teer.

Accounts and gameplay

You may play as a guest or register with a username and six-digit PIN. Registration does not require an email address. We store your username, a protected PIN hash, recovery-code hash, account creation time, and sign-in session records. We do not store the PIN or recovery code in plain text in the account database.

When you save a result, we store information needed for scores, rankings, and history, such as the gamertag, game mode, run identifier, score, round grades or answers where available, and saved time or challenge date. The service processes your submitted map guesses to calculate distances. Map guesses are game answers, not measurements of your device's physical location.

Public leaderboards and username claims

Submitted leaderboard gamertags and results are public. Daily results can be found using a gamertag and challenge date and can include round grades and ranking information. Choose a username that does not reveal personal information you want to keep private.

An available, previously unregistered guest gamertag can be claimed when creating an account. Stored results under that matching gamertag can then be linked to the account. Guest gamertags did not have verified ownership before registration.

Daily Challenge replays

New Daily results retain each submitted map guess, tornado details, round distance, and grade for Replay Mode. Signed-in players who have completed and saved that same day’s Daily Challenge can view these replays. Replay Mode does not provide share or export controls; it cannot guarantee that viewers will not capture or redistribute what is displayed.

Storage on your device

The website uses browser storage for guest results, preferences, Daily progress, and sign-in tokens. The app uses persistent web storage and device preferences for its session, sound and display settings, and unfinished-run recovery. App run snapshots are eligible for recovery for up to 24 hours; an expired snapshot may remain locally until replaced or cleared. Clearing browser/app data can remove local progress and sign-in information. Online results are separate from local storage.

Feedback and support

If you submit feedback, the form processes the message, selections, and any contact details you choose to provide. Feedback is sent through Formspree. Do not include your PIN, recovery code, or other sensitive information in feedback.

Infrastructure and outside services

GitHub Pages hosts website resources. Cloudflare Workers and D1 provide game APIs and online storage. Formspree processes feedback. Map tiles are requested from OpenStreetMap, and some web libraries are loaded from unpkg. These providers receive network information such as IP addresses and requested resources when you connect to their services, and may keep operational logs under their own policies. The account service also uses hashed network-address and username identifiers for sign-in attempt limits.

External event reports, image credits, and other linked sites have their own privacy practices. Relevant provider information: GitHub, Cloudflare, Formspree, and OpenStreetMap Foundation.

How information is used

We use the information described here to operate sign-in, calculate and display scores, recover unfinished games, synchronize history, handle feedback, and limit misuse of the service. Sharing a result through your device's share controls sends the selected content to the destination you choose.

Retention and your choices

Online account and score records do not currently have a general automatic deletion schedule; they may remain until removed by the operators. Sign-in sessions are configured to expire after 30 days. Temporary game-session and sign-in-limit records have shorter expiry periods and may be removed during later service requests. Provider logs and feedback records follow the providers' and operators' retention practices.

You can sign out and clear local browser/app data. To request access, correction, or deletion of an account or stored results, contact us through the feedback form, select the appropriate topic, and identify the gamertag and request. We may ask for information to verify the request without asking for your PIN. Removing local app data or uninstalling the app does not itself delete online scores.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the service changes. The date above identifies the latest revision. For privacy questions, use Tornado Guesser feedback.
